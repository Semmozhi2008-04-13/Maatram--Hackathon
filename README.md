## Problem Statement

Maatram manages a student's journey from **application to student life, graduation, alumni, and career**. Currently, this information is scattered across different systems, forms, and spreadsheets, making it difficult to maintain a single, accurate record of each student.

This can lead to **duplicate records, inconsistent information, and loss of important history**.

The goal of this project is to build a **unified Student Lifecycle Platform** that maintains one trustworthy record for each person and connects their complete journey in Maatram.

The platform will support:

- **Admissions** — application, draft saving, review, shortlisting, selection, and rejection.
- **Student Lifecycle** — linking selected applicants to their student records and maintaining their history.
- **Event History** — recording important changes as events that can be safely replayed.
- **Alumni Career Tracking** — maintaining career history and finding alumni by their current company.
- **Smart Document Uploads** — securely uploading and validating documents using S3-compatible storage.
- **Auditability** — keeping a clear history of important status changes and actions.

The prototype will use **synthetic data only** and will not contain real student information.
